FAQ

Director's commentary.

00:12Will the ads look “AI-generated”?

Not if we do our job. Every ad gets a human review before you see it, and we fix anything that looks off: hands, text, motion or brand colours.

00:31Why so many versions of one ad?

Nobody knows in advance which hook will work, not even big agencies. Testing several angles at once is the fastest and cheapest way to find the one that does. Then we make more like it.

00:47What do you need from me?

Your product or service, the offer, who you sell to, and any brand assets you have: logo, colours, product photos, past ads. If you have none, we'll work from your website or Instagram.

01:05Do you run the ads too?

We make the creatives and suggest copy and a testing plan for Meta Ads Manager. If you want help setting up campaigns, ask and we'll scope it.

01:22Who owns the ads?

You do. Once a pack is paid for, the final videos are yours to run on any platform for as long as you like.

01:40Can you match my existing brand?

Yes. We follow your fonts, colours, tone and any do's and don'ts, and keep a brand sheet so every month's ads stay consistent.
